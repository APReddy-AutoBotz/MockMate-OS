# Product requirements

## Product outcome

The user supplies content they own or are authorized to adapt. The system helps turn it into a polished video using an approved voice profile and avatar, while the user edits and approves every stage. The final result is an MP4 for manual download and publishing.

## Primary user

An individual creator or small team producing explainers, product updates, learning content, project demonstrations, or social videos without repeatedly recording camera and voice footage.

## V1 scope

- Project dashboard and stage timeline
- Content paste/upload and rights attestation
- Script drafting adapter, editing, versioning and approval
- Voice-profile consent, private sample management, generation and approval
- Avatar-profile consent, private sample management, generation and approval
- B-roll, captions, aspect ratio and brand settings
- Preview and final render
- Download of approved MP4, captions and thumbnail frame
- Full audit trail and deletion

## Out of scope

- YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn or TikTok publishing
- Autonomous approval
- Scraping or adapting third-party content without an attestation
- Cloning another person's voice or likeness without verified consent
- Training foundation models
- Keeping biometric samples indefinitely
- Using the ChatGPT website as an automated backend

## Acceptance principle

A generated artifact is only a draft. It cannot unlock the next stage until the owner reviews the exact immutable version and approves it.
